02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE CARDS

魔术师 · 张君琛

Hi, I’m Jack.
Pick a card.

I fell in love with magic as a kid watching David Copperfield on TV. A few magic toys turned into performances for classmates, a university magic club, and eventually a life spent finding new ways to surprise people.

I’ve performed more than 100 shows since turning professional in 2013, from corporate events for Google, Facebook, Amazon and Robinhood to TEDx, Stanford galas and the DNA Comedy Club stage.

Through Entire Productions, I’ve also brought close-up magic to celebrations for Genentech, Moloco and Paula LeDuc Fine Catering & Events.

Cards are my first love. But whether I’m reading a mind or making something appear, the idea is the same: make people laugh, bring them together, and leave them with a story.
